# Product Foundation

## Objective

Build a verified restaurant platform for Azerbaijan covering discovery, QR menus, table ordering, kitchen operations, and provider-backed payments.

## Pilot milestone

An approved restaurant can create its location, publish an approved multilingual menu, invite staff, generate revocable table QR codes, receive test orders, and advance those orders through the kitchen workflow.

## Initial user surfaces

- Customer: responsive web/PWA and iOS/Android app.
- Restaurant owner: responsive web interface for laptop and tablet.
- Kitchen: tablet/web kiosk interface.
- Platform operations: restricted web interface.

## Rules

- Publish only restaurant-approved data.
- Ratings must come from eligible, completed customer interactions.
- Prices, totals, permissions, order transitions, and payments are server-authoritative.
- The platform must not hold or redistribute restaurant funds without the required licensed-provider structure and legal approval.

## Pilot non-goals

- Delivery logistics
- Loyalty and advertising
- Public reviews
- Reservations
- AI recommendations
- POS integrations unless required by a pilot restaurant
- Separate Windows or macOS store applications
